Questions

Frequently Asked

Find answers to common questions about our programs

Is Brazilian Jiu Jitsu safe? +

Yes. Safety is our top priority. We teach proper technique to prevent injury, tap out systems allow students to control intensity, and our instructors supervise all training. Beginners start with fundamental techniques before progressing to advanced movements.

Do I need to be in shape to start? +

No. Jiu Jitsu is perfect for all fitness levels. We'll modify exercises to match your ability, and you build strength and endurance as you train. Many of our students started with zero athletic background.

What age can kids start? +

Our Little Dragons program starts at age 4-6, emphasizing coordination and basic skills through games. Mini Competitors (7-10) learn fundamental techniques. Teens (11-15) progress toward competition-level training. Adults can start at any age.

How long until I get a blue belt? +

Promotion depends on technical skill, consistency, and time. Most dedicated students earn their blue belt (1st promotion) after 6-18 months of regular training. Prof. Leandro evaluates each student individually and promotes based on readiness.

What's the difference between Gi and No-Gi? +

Gi training uses a traditional kimono with collar and sleeves, allowing grip-based techniques. No-Gi uses athletic wear and emphasizes footlock and grip-free grappling. Both are valuable; mastering both makes you a well-rounded practitioner.

How much does a gi cost? +

Quality gis range from $80-120. We offer Castroviejo branded gis, or you can buy elsewhere. Loaner gis are available at no charge while you decide.

Is there a long-term contract? +

No. We offer month-to-month memberships with no long-term contract. You can pause for up to 3 months or cancel anytime without penalty. The only requirement is that you're good to yourself and your training partners.

Can I watch a class before joining? +

Absolutely. We welcome potential students to observe classes. Just let staff know when you arrive. After class, feel free to ask Prof. Leandro or other instructors any questions. Your first class to participate is also free.

What's the academy like? +

Castroviejo BJJ is a welcoming, inclusive community. We celebrate each other's progress regardless of belt level. Prof. Leandro creates an environment where ego is left at the door, everyone is supportive, and training is both challenging and fun.

Do you offer private lessons? +

Yes. Private lessons with Prof. Leandro are available for students who want personalized instruction. Contact us to discuss pricing and availability. Privates are great for addressing specific techniques or accelerating progress.

Do you have competition opportunities? +

Yes. We encourage interested students to compete in IBJJF tournaments. Prof. Leandro provides competition preparation training. Competition is optional, but highly rewarding for those interested in testing their skills.

What if I get injured? +

Tell your instructor about any injuries before class. We'll modify techniques to accommodate you. If you have a serious injury, rest until you're fully recovered. Come back when you're ready. Your health comes first.
