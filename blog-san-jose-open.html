San Jose IBJJF International Open Success

January 27, 2026 · 1 min read · By Chris Jr

We're proud to share some outstanding results from the San Jose IBJJF Winter International Open, held on January 17th, 2026.

Prof. Leandro Castroviejo

Prof. Leandro competed in the Masters division and delivered a dominant performance, taking gold in both divisions he entered:

Gold – Ultra Heavy, Masters
Gold – Open Class, Masters

A strong showing at a major IBJJF event, reflecting experience, composure, and high-level jiu-jitsu under pressure.

Prof. Daniel (Mao de Broca)

We're also excited to congratulate Prof. Daniel, who had an exceptional weekend of his own, winning gold in both of his divisions:

Gold – Master 3 / Black / Ultra-Heavy
Gold – Master 3 / Black / Open Class

Although the IBJJF results list him under his previous affiliation, Prof. Daniel has recently joined Castroviejo Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu as a full-time Professor and competed at the event wearing the Castroviejo gi.

What's Next

The competition doesn't stop there. Prof. Leandro and Prof. Daniel will both be back on the mats competing at the Los Angeles Winter International Open IBJJF Jiu-Jitsu Championship, taking place January 31 through February 1 at the Los Angeles Convention Center.

Two Professors. Four gold medals. On to the next challenge. Oss!
