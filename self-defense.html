Specialized Program

Self Defense

Practical, Effective, Real-World Techniques

Learn Real Self-Defense

Our self-defense program focuses on practical techniques you can actually use in real-world situations. Unlike traditional martial arts that emphasize forms and philosophy, Brazilian Jiu Jitsu is centered on what works when it matters.

Prof. Leandro teaches escape and control techniques that work regardless of size or strength. The program emphasizes awareness, de-escalation, and practical skills that build confidence and real safety.

No experience necessary. Classes accommodate all fitness levels and learning speeds. You'll train in a supportive, judgment-free environment where safety is always the priority.

What You Learn

Practical Techniques

Escape & Defense

Learn how to escape from dangerous positions and defend against common attacks with proven techniques.

Control & Positioning

Master positions that give you safety and control, allowing you to neutralize threats effectively.

Awareness & Prevention

Develop situational awareness skills that help you avoid dangerous situations before they happen.

Confidence Building

Real training builds real confidence. Know you can protect yourself and move through the world safely.

Size Doesn't Matter

Brazilian Jiu Jitsu principles work regardless of size or strength. Technique trumps power every time.

Age Appropriate

Self-defense concepts work for everyone from teenagers to seniors. Our curriculum adapts to your needs.
