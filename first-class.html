Getting Started

Your First Class

A step-by-step guide to make your first experience smooth and comfortable

Before Class

Prepare for Success

Choose Your Class

Visit our schedule and pick a time that works for you. We recommend a morning or fundamentals class for beginners.

What to Bring

Bring athletic clothes or a gi (if you have one), water bottle, towel, and a positive attitude. Loaner gis provided free of charge.

Arrive 15 Minutes Early

This gives you time to settle in, meet instructors, and ask questions. We'll have you all set before class starts.

Tell Us You're New

Let Prof. Leandro or an instructor know it's your first class. We'll give you extra attention and modify techniques as needed.

During Class

What to Expect

1 Warmup (5 min)

Light cardio and dynamic stretching to get your body ready. No prior experience needed.

2 Technique Instruction and Drilling (30 min)

Prof. Leandro teaches fundamental movements step-by-step. You'll practice the techniques with a partner, repeating movements to build muscle memory. We'll pair you with a friendly, experienced student.

3 Open Mat / Rolling (20 min)

Optional light rolling at your comfort level. You can also drill more or stretch instead. No pressure to participate.

4 Cooldown (5 min)

Stretching and cooldown. Class ends with a bow to show respect for the art and your training partners.

Tips & Etiquette

Class Guidelines

Dress Code

For Gi Classes: Gi (loaner gis available at no charge), no shoes on mat

For No-Gi Classes: Athletic shorts/pants, rash guard or t-shirt, no shoes on mat

Safety Tips

- Tell your instructor about any injuries or health concerns

- Tap early if something hurts (no shame in tapping)

- Keep fingernails and toenails trimmed

- We don't do knee reaping on white belts (specific to BJJ)

Training Etiquette

- Be on time and dressed by start time

- Bow to instructors and training partners

- Don't correct other students (let instructors teach)

- Control your strength with less experienced students

Hygiene

- Clean gi and body before class

- No strong perfume or cologne

- Wash gi after every class

Common Questions

First-Timer FAQ

I've never done martial arts. Will I be lost? +

No! We teach fundamentals from the ground up. Everyone starts as a beginner. The most important thing is showing up with an open mind and willingness to learn.

Do I need to buy a gi right away? +

No. Loaner gis are provided free of charge, or you can wear athletic clothes for No-Gi classes. Try it out first, then invest in your own gi when you're ready.

What if I'm not in great shape? +

Jiu Jitsu works for all fitness levels. We'll modify techniques and pace to match your fitness. It's an excellent way to build strength, cardiovascular health, and flexibility.

Will I get hurt? +

We prioritize safety above all. You control how hard you train. If something hurts, tap and the movement stops. Beginners roll gently and always have the choice to sit out.

Can I watch a class before joining? +

Of course! We welcome potential students to observe. Just ask staff when you arrive, and feel free to ask questions afterward.

What's the atmosphere like? +

Welcoming and supportive. We're a tight-knit community that celebrates each other's progress. Ego is left at the door. You'll make friends instantly.
