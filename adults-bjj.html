Adult BJJ Classes

Master the Art of Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Gi and No-Gi Training

Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced practitioner, our adult BJJ program is designed to meet you where you are. We offer both Gi and No-Gi classes for all skill levels, allowing you to develop well-rounded grappling skills.

Learn traditional Brazilian Jiu Jitsu techniques from an IBJJF certified 3rd Degree Black Belt. Our curriculum covers takedowns, guard work, submissions, positional control, and practical self-defense principles that apply in real-world situations.

Training Essentials

What You'll Learn

Fundamental Techniques

Master the building blocks: proper stance, posture, movement, and basic submissions that form the foundation of effective Jiu Jitsu.

Guard Mastery

Develop strong guard positions including closed guard, open guard, and half guard to control opponents and create submission opportunities.

Positional Control

Learn dominant positions like mount, side control, and back control to maintain advantage and finish opponents with control.

Submission Systems

Progress through submission chains and transitions, learning joint locks and chokes that complement your positional game.

Takedown Defense

Develop the skills to stay safe standing and recognize when to pull guard or pivot away from danger.

Problem Solving

Apply your knowledge through drilling and live rolling, developing the adaptability that makes you successful in any situation.
