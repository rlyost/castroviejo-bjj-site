BJJ in Redwood City and the Mid-Peninsula

Serving Redwood City, Menlo Park, San Carlos, Belmont, and East Palo Alto

Redwood City's Home for Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Castroviejo Brazilian Jiu Jitsu Academy is located at 701 Arguello Street in the heart of Redwood City. We teach authentic Gi and No-Gi Brazilian Jiu Jitsu to students of all ages and experience levels, from first-time beginners to competitive athletes.

Our academy sits in one of the most accessible locations on the mid-Peninsula. Families and individuals from Menlo Park, San Carlos, Belmont, and East Palo Alto train here regularly because of the short commute and the quality of instruction. Most of our students drive less than 10 minutes to reach the mat.

Lead instructor Leandro Castroviejo is an IBJJF certified 3rd Degree Black Belt with over 20 years of competition and coaching experience. He has won more than 50 medals at the highest levels of the sport. When you train at Castroviejo BJJ, you learn from someone who has tested these techniques against world-class opponents and refined them for students at every stage.

Easy to Reach

How to Get to Castroviejo BJJ

Our academy at 701 Arguello Street is centrally located in Redwood City with easy access from Highway 101 and El Camino Real. Free parking is available on site.

From Menlo Park

Head south on El Camino Real or take Middlefield Road. The drive is about 7 minutes depending on traffic. Menlo Park residents make up a large part of our adult evening classes.

From San Carlos

Take El Camino Real north or hop on 101. You'll be on the mat in under 8 minutes. Several of our competition team members train from San Carlos.

From Belmont

Head south on El Camino Real or Ralston Avenue to 101. Roughly a 10-minute drive. Belmont families often pair our Saturday kids No-Gi classes with weekend errands in downtown Redwood City.

From East Palo Alto

Take University Avenue to 101 South, exit Woodside Road. About 8 minutes door to door. Our after-school kids programs are a convenient option for East Palo Alto families.

Why Train With Us

What Sets Castroviejo BJJ Apart

World-Class Instruction

Prof. Leandro Castroviejo brings 20+ years of experience and 50+ competition medals. He personally teaches the majority of classes, meaning you get direct access to elite-level coaching every session.

Structured Curriculum

Our programs follow a clear progression system. You know what you're working toward, from your first day as a white belt through advanced competition preparation. No guessing, no random techniques.

Welcoming Community

Students from Redwood City, Menlo Park, San Carlos, Belmont, and East Palo Alto train together in a supportive, ego-free environment. New students are paired with experienced training partners to accelerate learning.

Flexible Schedule

With morning, afternoon, evening, and Saturday classes, you can find a time that fits your life. Many members train multiple times per week because the schedule makes it possible.

Competition Opportunities

Our students compete in IBJJF tournaments and local events throughout the Bay Area. Recent results include multiple gold medals at the San Jose Open and strong showings at the Pan Americans.

Free Trial Class

Your first class costs nothing. Walk in, meet the team, try the training. No pressure, no contracts to sign before you step on the mat. See for yourself why families across the mid-Peninsula choose us.
