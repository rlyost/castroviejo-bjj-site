Programs

Kids BJJ Classes

Building Confidence, Discipline, and Skills

Age Groups

Programs for Every Age

Little Dragons

Ages 4-6

Introduction to Jiu Jitsu through play and games. We focus on coordination, listening skills, and building confidence in a fun, supportive environment. Families from Menlo Park and East Palo Alto love this after-school program for its convenient timing and structured approach.

Mini Competitors

Ages 7-10

Fundamental techniques with increased intensity. Students learn basic positions, submissions, and develop competitive spirit while building friendships.

Teens

Ages 11-15

Advanced techniques and competition preparation. Our teen program bridges childhood and adult training with proper technique emphasis. Teens from San Carlos and Belmont train alongside our Redwood City students in a focused, growth-oriented environment.

Why Jiu Jitsu for Kids

Benefits That Last a Lifetime

Builds Confidence

As students progress and earn belts, they develop self-assurance that extends to school and life.

Teaches Discipline

Jiu Jitsu requires respect, focus, and commitment. These values translate to academics and personal behavior.

Anti-Bullying Training

Self-defense skills and confidence prevent bullying. Kids learn to stand up for themselves and others.

Physical Fitness

Jiu Jitsu builds strength, flexibility, and cardiovascular health in an enjoyable, social setting.
