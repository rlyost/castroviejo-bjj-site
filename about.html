Leandro Castroviejo

My Story

I grew up in Sao Paulo, Brazil. If you know anything about Sao Paulo, you know it's a city that teaches you to be tough. I found Jiu Jitsu as a kid, and it gave me something I didn't know I was missing: focus, respect, and a way to channel all that energy into something productive. From the first time I stepped on the mat, I knew this was going to be my life.

I trained obsessively. I competed everywhere I could. I earned medals at the IBJJF World Championships, won the Pan Americans, took gold at the US Open four times, won the American Cup twice, and became a multiple-time National Champion. In 2016, I received my black belt. In 2026, I earned a silver medal at the Pan Ams, because I still believe a coach should do what he asks his students to do: show up, compete, and put it all on the line.

When I moved to the United States, I spent six years training and teaching within the Charles Gracie network. Those years shaped me as an instructor. I learned how to run a program, how to build a team, and how to pass knowledge to students in a way that sticks. I'm proud of my connection to the Gracie family and the lineage I carry every time I step on the mat.

But I wanted something of my own. I wanted to build a place where the culture felt right from day one. That's why I chose Redwood City. This community deserved an academy where adults and kids could train real Jiu Jitsu in a clean, welcoming environment with a coach who knows them by name.

Here's what I believe about coaching: technique matters, but it's not enough. I've seen Jiu Jitsu change people's confidence, their fitness, their ability to handle stress. I've watched shy kids become leaders. I've seen women walk in nervous about self-defense and walk out knowing they can protect themselves. That transformation is what drives me. Every class I teach, I'm trying to give you what Jiu Jitsu gave me growing up in Sao Paulo: discipline, confidence, and a community that has your back.

I'm also a father. My kids keep me grounded and remind me why this work matters. I want every student who walks through my door to feel like family. Whether you're a white belt who's never grappled or a competitor chasing your next medal, you belong here.

Come try a free class. I'll be the one on the mat.
