Our Instructor

Leandro Castroviejo

IBJJF 3rd Degree Black Belt

A Lifetime of Jiu Jitsu Mastery

Professor Leandro Castroviejo was born and raised in Sao Paulo, Brazil, where his journey with Brazilian Jiu Jitsu began. From an early age, he was fascinated by the discipline, respect, and physicality that BJJ demands. After moving to the United States, he continued to pursue his passion, earning his black belt in 2016.

As a multiple-time National Champion, 2x American Cup Champion, and 4x US Open Champion, Prof. Leandro has competed at the highest levels of the sport, including the IBJJF World Championships and Pan American Championship. In 2026, he earned a Silver Medal at the Pan American Championships, proving that his technical knowledge continues to be backed by real-world competitive success.

With over 12 years of teaching experience, Prof. Leandro focuses on technique, confidence, discipline, and overall well-being. He believes BJJ has the power to transform lives and is particularly passionate about teaching self-defense to women and teenage girls.

After 6 years with Charles Gracie academies, Prof. Leandro branched out to create Castroviejo Brazilian Jiu Jitsu Academy. He is proud to represent the Gracie legacy while building a supportive community for students of all ages and backgrounds in Redwood City, CA.
