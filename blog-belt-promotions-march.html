Belt Promotion Ceremony – March 22nd, 2025

March 26, 2025 · 1 min read · By Chris Jr

We were delighted to host a belt promotion ceremony for both children and adults on March 22, 2025. It was a fantastic day to come together as a team and celebrate the hard work, dedication, and progress of our academy members.

From our youngest grapplers to seasoned practitioners, every promotion marked a milestone earned through countless hours on the mat, resilience, and commitment to growth. The atmosphere was filled with energy, support, and a deep sense of pride as teammates, friends, and family members cheered each step forward.

Congratulations to everyone who leveled up. Whether it was your first stripe or a brand-new belt, each promotion is a reflection of your discipline and perseverance. We're proud of every one of you, and we can't wait to see what you accomplish next.

Keep training hard. Oss!
